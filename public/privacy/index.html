Privacy

F.Tarot is provided by Songhai Fan, an individual based in Australia. We do not store your questions, readings or personal profiles in an application database. Your question and card state are processed only to provide the current experience; restoration state is carried in session handles rather than a saved reading history.

When you use the ChatGPT plugin, your question, revealed cards and requested interpretation are shared with the current ChatGPT conversation. Conversation and file retention follow OpenAI's policies and your account controls. Session snapshots are valid for seven days; this is not a deletion period for ChatGPT conversations or files.

Language and artwork preferences may be stored in your browser until you clear this site's storage. Save Result creates a PNG on your device or, when supported, in your ChatGPT file library. You control deleting or sharing those copies.

Vercel hosts the website and MCP service; infrastructure providers may process technical request records to operate the service. Hosting logs follow the provider's applicable retention settings (Vercel runtime-log limits). Google Fonts serves the card table's fonts. These providers' processing is separate from F.Tarot's application storage.

If you email songhai.fan2022@gmail.com, your address, message and attachments are used only to handle your request, through Gmail. Support correspondence is retained while needed to handle that request; you can ask for it to be deleted. Please avoid sending credentials or unnecessary personal details.

隐私

F.Tarot 由位于澳大利亚的个人发布者范松海（Songhai Fan）提供。我们不将你的问题、读牌结果或个人档案保存到应用数据库。问题和卡牌状态仅用于提供当前体验；恢复状态由会话凭证携带，不建立读牌历史库。

网站和 MCP 服务由 Vercel 托管，基础设施提供者可能为运行服务处理技术请求记录。托管日志遵循服务商适用的保留设置（Vercel 日志保留说明）。牌桌使用 Google Fonts 提供字体。这些处理与 F.Tarot 自身的应用存储分别管理。

如果邮件联系 songhai.fan2022@gmail.com，邮箱地址、邮件和附件仅通过 Gmail 用于处理你的请求。支持邮件在处理请求所需期间保留，你可以要求删除。请勿发送凭证或不必要的个人资料。
